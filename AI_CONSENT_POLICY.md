# AI_CONSENT_POLICY.md

## Purpose

This policy explains consent requirements for:
- Session recording
- Gemini AI lesson analysis
- AI-generated educational reports

---

## Consent Requirement

AI analysis requires explicit consent before recording or processing any session.

Consent must be provided by:
- Tutor
- Student
- Parent/guardian if student is under 18

Without consent:
- Sessions must not be recorded
- Sessions must not be analyzed by Gemini
- AI reports must not be generated

---

## AI Usage

Gemini may be used to:
- Summarize lessons
- Identify covered topics
- Identify improvement areas
- Generate educational recommendations

The AI system is not:
- A medical tool
- A psychological tool
- A grading authority

---

## Report Usage

AI reports are:
- Educational assistance summaries
- Intended for tutors, students, and parents
- Generated automatically

Reports may contain inaccuracies.

Users should not rely on reports as professional diagnosis.

---

## Recording Rules

Recordings:
- Are private
- Are not publicly accessible
- Must be stored securely
- May be deleted according to platform policies

---

## Data Protection

The platform will:
- Restrict access to recordings
- Protect AI reports
- Avoid unauthorized sharing
- Use secure processing providers where possible

---

## Withdrawal of Consent

Users may withdraw future consent for recording and AI analysis.

Previously generated reports may remain stored for operational or legal reasons.
