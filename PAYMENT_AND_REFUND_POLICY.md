# PAYMENT_AND_REFUND_POLICY.md

## Payment Provider

Payments are processed through Tap Payments.

The platform does not support:
- Cash payments
- PayPal
- Stripe
- Cryptocurrency

---

## Booking Payment Flow

1. Student requests booking
2. Tutor accepts or rejects
3. Student pays through Tap Payments
4. Booking becomes confirmed
5. Lesson takes place

---

## Refund Policy

### Student Cancellation

- Before 24 hours:
  Full refund

- Between 12 and 24 hours:
  50% refund

- Less than 12 hours:
  No refund

### Tutor Cancellation

- Student receives full refund

---

## Failed Payments

If payment fails:
- Booking remains unconfirmed
- Student may retry payment

---

## Withdrawal Requests

Tutors may request withdrawals:
- Minimum amount: 100 QAR
- Subject to admin approval

---

## Fraud Prevention

The platform may:
- Delay suspicious transactions
- Review payment activity
- Suspend accounts involved in fraud

---

## Disputes

Users may contact support regarding:
- Payment disputes
- Refund issues
- Booking conflicts

The platform reserves the right to review disputes individually.

---

## Chargebacks

Fraudulent or abusive chargebacks may result in:
- Account suspension
- Permanent ban
- Legal escalation where necessary
