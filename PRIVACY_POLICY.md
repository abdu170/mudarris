# PRIVACY_POLICY.md

## Purpose

This policy explains how مُدرّس collects and uses user data.

---

## Data Collected

The platform may collect:
- Name
- Email
- Phone number
- Booking information
- Messages
- Payment metadata
- Session recordings (with consent)
- AI-generated lesson summaries

---

## How Data Is Used

Data may be used for:
- Account management
- Booking management
- Payments
- Tutor approvals
- Customer support
- AI lesson reports
- Security and fraud prevention

---

## Session Recordings

Online lesson recordings:
- Require consent
- May be analyzed by Gemini for educational summaries
- Are not publicly accessible

Recordings are stored securely.

---

## AI Processing

If consent is approved:
- Session recordings may be processed by Gemini
- Gemini may generate educational insights
- Reports are shared only with authorized users

AI reports are educational assistance tools only.

---

## Data Sharing

The platform may share limited data with:
- Tap Payments
- Merithub
- Twilio
- Resend
- Google Gemini

Only data necessary for platform functionality is shared.

---

## User Rights

Users may request:
- Access to their data
- Correction of incorrect data
- Account deletion requests
- Removal of recordings where applicable

---

## Security

The platform uses:
- HTTPS
- Access controls
- Secure cloud storage
- Authentication protections

However, no online platform can guarantee absolute security.

---

## Children

If a student is under 18:
- Parent/guardian consent may be required
- Recording and AI analysis require consent

---

## Policy Updates

The platform may update this policy when needed.
