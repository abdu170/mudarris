## Review Rules

Only students with completed paid lessons can leave reviews.

A student can leave:
- one review per completed booking

Reviews include:
- rating (1-5)
- optional written comment
- category ratings

Review categories:
- teaching quality
- punctuality
- communication
- lesson usefulness

Tutor rating average should update automatically after approved reviews.

Do not allow anonymous reviews.

---

## Tutor Card Rating Display

Tutor cards must display:
- rating average
- total review count

Format example:
⭐ 4.8 (124)
